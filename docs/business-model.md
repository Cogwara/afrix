# AFRIX Business Model & Economics

## Core Ethical Principle

> **AFRIX is a real work marketplace.** Workers earn from completing legitimate business-funded tasks. It is not an investment platform and must not depend on user deposits to pay previous users.

## 1. Revenue Streams

1. **Enterprise Campaign Platform Fee**:
   - AFRIX charges businesses a transparent platform fee (typically 10%) on total campaign budgets.
   - For example: A business funding a $500 retail audit campaign allocates $450 to worker rewards and $50 as the platform service fee.
2. **Enterprise SLA & Custom QA Audits**:
   - High-throughput AI clients receive dedicated data engineering, custom taxonomy curation, and consensus cross-validation pipelines.
3. **Data Delivery API Subscriptions (Phase 2)**:
   - Streaming consumer pricing feeds and real-time open-air retail market analytics.

## 2. Worker Value Proposition

- **Zero Barrier to Entry**: No registration fees, membership upgrades, or equipment purchases.
- **Fair African Compensation**: Workers are paid in benchmarked USD rewards ($0.08 to $3.50+ per microtask) and cash out to local currency (NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR) or mobile money wallets.
- **Merit-Based Progression**: Transparent XP levels from Newbie to Pro reward diligence, accuracy, and punctuality.
